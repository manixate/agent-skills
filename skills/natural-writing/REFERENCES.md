# References

These sources support the skill's rules. They are background, not required reading.

- Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" (WikiProject AI Cleanup): examples used in the artificial-writing categories.
- William Zinsser, *On Writing Well*: cut clutter, prefer plain and warm.
- Joseph Williams, *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace*: characters as subjects, actions as verbs.
- Richard Lanham, *Revising Prose*: the Paramedic Method for passive voice and noun-strings.
- Steven Pinker, *The Sense of Style*: the curse of knowledge, and why clear prose serves the reader.
- Strunk and White, *The Elements of Style*: concise composition rules, including "omit needless words."
