# References

These sources support the skill's rules. They are background, not required reading.

- Malte Ubl, "Design Docs at Google" (industrial-empathy.com): problem first, options and tradeoffs, a clear decision, and the cross-cutting concerns most designs skip.
- Oxide Computer's RFD process (rfd.shared.oxide.computer): a public example of recording decisions and reasoning over time.
- Gergely Orosz, "Scaling Engineering Teams via Writing Things Down: RFCs": planning, documentation, senior sign-off, and organization-wide review, with artifact details adapted to the system.
- Will Larson, staffeng.com "Engineering Strategy" and lethain.com: the relationship between design and strategy and the "gather together, write alone" authorship model.
- Caitie McCaffrey, "Design Docs, Markdown, and Git": pull-request review, explicit comment resolution, and merge as approval.
- Stitch Fix, "Making Technical Decisions when Your Team is Remote": asynchronous review and separating premise decisions from later detail.
- Michael Nygard, "Documenting Architecture Decisions": the context, decision, and consequences of a technical choice.
- Barbara Minto, *The Pyramid Principle*: the answer-first summary.
- Amazon's one-way / two-way door framing: rigor scales with reversibility.
- Simon Brown's C4 model (c4model.com) for diagrams at context, container, component, and code levels. Use a diagram format supported by the target repository and publishing system.
